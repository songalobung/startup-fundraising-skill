# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve the Startup Fundraising Skill for Codex. Contributions should make the workflow more useful, accurate, or easier to apply across different company stages, business models, and jurisdictions.

## Before you contribute

- Open an issue for substantial changes so the scope can be discussed first.
- Do not post private company, investor, customer, personal, or financial information in public issues or pull requests. Use fictional or fully anonymized examples.
- For factual or regulatory changes, include direct, authoritative sources and the date checked. Explain the source's jurisdiction and limits.
- Keep fundraising guidance educational. Do not add personalized legal, tax, accounting, or investment recommendations.

## Pull requests

Keep changes focused and update the relevant source references when guidance changes. In your pull request, describe:

1. The problem the change addresses.
2. The behavior or wording that changed.
3. Sources consulted, with access dates, for factual claims.
4. Any validation performed and known limitations.

Do not include secrets, private datasets, credentials, or personal information. By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be distributed under the repository's MIT License.
